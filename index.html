Shop Trend Research Tool

A private, read-only research tool for one user.

What it does

This tool uses Etsy's read-only API to look up public shop and listing data, such as shop open date, total sales, and listing details. It helps the owner see which newer shops are selling well and which product types are in demand.

What it does not do

  • It does not create, edit, or delete listings.
  • It does not access buyer, order, or payment data.
  • It does not post, message, or take any action on Etsy.

Who has access

Only the app owner. Results are saved privately on the owner's own computer. No data is shared, sold, or shown to anyone else.
